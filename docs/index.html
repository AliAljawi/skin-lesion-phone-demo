      About this model

      On the external DDI set (clinical photos, all skin tones), the group's MobileNetV3-Large reached a malignancy AUC of 0.60, against 0.91 on its own test split. Treat phone photos of new patients with that drop in mind.

      Training data: ISIC 2019 (CC BY-NC 4.0) and PAD-UFES-20 (CC BY 4.0).

      For teaching only. It cannot diagnose skin cancer; see a doctor about any lesion that worries you.
